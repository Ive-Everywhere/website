---
title: Start every req from what worked
description: Draft the job description and sourcing brief from past successful hires, not a blank page.
publishedAt: 2026-08-19
icon: ri-file-text-line
category: People
collection: Hiring
featured: false
order: 60
difficulty: starter
skills:
  - name: Past-hire calibration
    description: Pull what worked from the last successful hires in this role
  - name: Bias-language check
    description: Screen the JD for gendered or exclusionary language before it ships
  - name: Sourcing brief format
    description: Target profiles, channels, and search strings for a sourcer
goal: draft a calibrated JD and sourcing brief from past-hire signal, not a blank page
integrations: [notion, googlesheets, gmail, slack]
whatItDoes:
  - Pulls what worked from the last successful hires in this role
  - Drafts an outcomes-based JD leveled against comparable roles
  - Screens the JD for bias language and rewrites what it flags
  - Drafts a sourcing brief with target profiles and search strings
howToUse:
  - Connect Notion, your hiring sheet, Gmail, and Slack to the agent
  - Set the trigger to a new req opening, or run it on demand
  - Paste the prompt and adjust the lookback and sign-off policy
  - Review the JD and brief before either one goes out the door
workflow:
  - kind: trigger
    title: New req opened
    body: Hiring manager submits the intake form.
  - kind: prompt
    title: Pull past-hire calibration
    body: Successful hires in this role or level, what worked.
  - kind: prompt
    title: Draft the JD
    body: Outcomes-based, bias-checked language, leveled to the role.
  - kind: result
    title: Deliver for review
    body: JD and sourcing brief posted to Slack, saved to the drive.
prompt: |
  Draft the job description and sourcing brief for the {{role}} req.

  1. Read the intake form for {{role}}: level, team, must-haves, and the
     hiring manager's notes.
  2. Pull the last {{lookback_hires}} successful hires at this level or role
     from {{ats_or_sheet}} and note what their JDs and sourcing criteria had
     in common.
  3. Draft the JD: outcomes-based responsibilities, must-have vs. nice-to-have,
     leveled against comparable roles.
  4. Run the JD through the bias-language check: flag and rewrite any
     gendered, exclusionary, or unnecessarily jargon-heavy phrasing before it
     ships.
  5. Draft the sourcing brief: 2-3 target company or profile archetypes,
     channels to search, and a starter Boolean string.
  6. Post the JD and sourcing brief to {{delivery_channel}} for the hiring
     manager's sign-off and save both to `hiring/{{role}}-{{date}}/`.

  Never post the JD to a job board or send the sourcing brief to a sourcer
  until the hiring manager approves it in {{delivery_channel}}.
---
