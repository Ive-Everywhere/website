    description: Isolate only what changed since the previous profile.

integrations: [drive, notion, slack]
whatItDoes:
  - Fetches live pricing, changelog, reviews, and job postings per competitor
  - Scores Delight, Hard-to-copy, Margin, every score tied to a cited URL
  - Diffs this month's findings against last month's saved profile
  - Re-fetches every load-bearing URL before publishing, no stale claims
howToUse:
  - Connect the drive, Notion, and the product Slack channel
  - Set the trigger for the 1st business day, 9:00 AM
  - Paste the prompt and set the standing watch list once
  - Review the diff and what it means before the roadmap review
workflow:
  - kind: trigger
    title: 1st business day, 9:00 AM
    body: Monthly refresh, one competitor or the full watch list.
  - kind: prompt
    title: Fetch the live sources
    body: Pricing, changelog, reviews, job postings, for each competitor.
  - kind: prompt
    title: Score through the DHM lens
    body: Delight, Hard-to-copy, Margin, each claim cited.
  - kind: prompt
    title: Diff against last month
    body: What changed since the last profile.
  - kind: result
    title: Publish + post
    body: Profile updated in the drive, diff summary to #product.
prompt: |
  Refresh the competitor profile for {{competitor}}.

  1. Fetch the live pricing page, changelog, recent reviews, and job
     postings for {{competitor}}.
  2. Score the competitor through the DHM lens (Delight, Hard-to-copy,
     Margin). Tie every score to a cited URL, not a general impression.
  3. Load last month's profile for {{competitor}} from
     `competitive/{{competitor}}.md` and diff this month's findings
     against it.
  4. Validation: re-fetch every URL cited as a load-bearing claim,
     pricing, a specific feature, to confirm it still says what the
     profile claims before publishing.
  5. Tie any meaningful change to the relevant roadmap item, if one
     exists, without editing the roadmap itself.
  6. Save the refreshed profile to `competitive/{{competitor}}.md` and
     post the diff, what changed, what it means for us, to #product.

  Do not restructure the roadmap from a competitive finding without my
  approval — surface the implication, don't act on it.
