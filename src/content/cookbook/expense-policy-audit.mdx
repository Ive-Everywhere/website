description: Check every expense report against policy, approve what's clean, and queue only genuine exceptions.

difficulty: starter
skills:
  - name: Expense policy reader
    description: Apply the written policy line by line, citing the clause for every call
  - name: Ambiguity escalation
    description: When policy language doesn't clearly cover a line, escalate rather than guess
  - name: Receipt-to-claim match
    description: Confirm the receipt in Drive actually supports the claimed amount and vendor
goal: check every expense line against policy and queue only the genuine exceptions
integrations: [googlesheets, gmail, drive, slack]
whatItDoes:
  - Matches every receipt to its claimed vendor, amount, and date first
  - Checks each line against the written policy and cites the exact clause
  - Approves only lines that pass both the receipt match and the policy check
  - Queues every exception with the policy clause and the reason attached
howToUse:
  - Connect the expense sheet, Drive receipts, Gmail, and Slack to the agent
  - Set it to run as new reports land, or on a daily sweep
  - Paste the prompt and answer the first-run questions, policy doc, approval limit, review channel
  - Review the exception queue in Slack; approved lines need no further action
workflow:
  - kind: trigger
    title: New expense report submitted
    body: Fires as reports land in the expense sheet or inbox.
  - kind: prompt
    title: Match receipt to claim
    body: Vendor, amount, and date checked against the attached receipt.
  - kind: prompt
    title: Check against policy
    body: Each line against the written policy, citing the clause.
  - kind: result
    title: Approve or queue
    body: Clean lines marked approved; exceptions posted with the citation.
prompt: |
  Audit expense reports submitted since the last run.

  1. Pull new expense reports from {{expense_sheet}}, with receipts from
     {{drive_folder}}.
  2. For each line item, check that the receipt matches the claimed vendor,
     amount, and date exactly.
  3. Check each line against {{policy_doc}}; cite the exact clause behind
     every approve or flag decision.
  4. Before you approve any line, confirm both checks passed, a policy clause
     was cited, and the receipt matches the claim exactly. If either check
     fails, move the line to the exception queue instead of approving it.
  5. Mark clean, in-policy, receipt-matched lines as approved in
     {{expense_sheet}}.
  6. Post the exception queue to {{review_channel}}: each line, the policy
     clause it triggered, and why it wasn't auto-approved.

  Do not approve a line the policy doesn't clearly cover, and do not issue
  reimbursement — approval marks the line ready, payment still needs
  sign-off.
